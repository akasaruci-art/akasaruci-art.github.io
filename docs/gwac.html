Glasgow Women's Art Collective

Glasgow Women's Art Collective is a group of women who love art and love making art. The group meets on a regular basis and supports one another in our creative pursuits.

Our next exhibition is at the New Glasgow Society Gallery, 1307 Argyle Street, Glasgow, G3 8TL, from Friday 27th November to Sunday 29th November. We would love you to come and see our work.
